# BetterSiquijor logo

The shared logo is `public/better-siquijor.svg`. The header, footer and favicon all use this asset. Edit the SVG to change the artwork; no runtime map request or image generation service is involved.

The white island follows the Siquijor polygon in `siquijor-natural-earth.geojson`, downloaded on 18 November 2026 from [GISGP’s Siquijor GeoJSON](https://gisgp-files.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/downloads/regions/philippines/siquijor/siquijor-boundary.geojson). GISGP identifies it as a conversion of Natural Earth’s 1:10m admin-1 data. [Natural Earth’s terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) place its map data in the public domain.

The polygon is displayed north-up, with longitude scaled by the cosine of the mean latitude. Its aspect ratio is preserved inside a 92 × 72 box, centred horizontally with its northernmost point at SVG y=22. Coordinates are rounded to two decimal places. This is a generalised coastline used as a brand illustration.

The palm, sun, waves and firefly sparkles are original vector artwork. They are decorative elements, not mapped locations. The logo uses the template’s blue, white and a gold accent.

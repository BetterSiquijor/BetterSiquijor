# Privacy

This edition has no accounts, application forms, advertising, or analytics scripts. Search works locally in your browser and does not send your search terms to a search server.

The hosting provider may process technical access logs. The template stores your display-language preference in browser local storage. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives normal technical request information.

## External services

Links to government websites and GitHub lead to services with their own privacy policies. BetterSiquijor does not handle or store government applications, health records, payment information, or identity documents.

## Corrections

The correction link opens GitHub. Nothing is submitted by BetterSiquijor. If you post a GitHub issue, it will be public. Please leave out personal, medical, financial, or identification details.

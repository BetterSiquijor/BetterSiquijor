# About BetterSiquijor

BetterSiquijor is an independent, volunteer-run portal for **Siquijor Province**. It helps residents find official services, provincial information, and original public records.

It is **not an official provincial or municipal government website** and does not represent those offices. It does not accept applications, collect fees, or determine eligibility for public services.

## Built on Better Local Gov

This site uses the [Better Local Gov starter by iyanski](https://github.com/iyanski/betterlocalgov), listed in the [BetterGov.ph community templates](https://lgu.bettergov.ph/TEMPLATES). The starter's blue theme, typography, navigation, cards, and footer are preserved. The content is customized for Siquijor.

## What is included

- Official service links with scope and source notes.
- Six municipality profiles with the **2024 POPCEN** population figures.
- Sourced provincial officials.
- Links to DILG financial disclosures, COA audit reports, and PhilGEPS procurement notices.
- A [source catalogue and review method](/government/sources).

## What is still being collected

Provincial budget figures, project progress, ordinances, additional officials, and verified office contacts are not yet compiled. Missing records do not mean zero spending or no government activity.

# First-time adult Philippine passport

Apply for your first Philippine passport as an adult. Prepare your documents, book an appointment, and check the application fees below.

## Bring to your appointment

- Confirmed online appointment, completed application form, and DFA payment e-receipt.
- Documentary proof of Philippine citizenship. For a natural-born applicant, this includes a PSA-issued Certificate of Live Birth or Report of Birth. The charter also identifies separate citizenship records for foundlings, naturalized citizens, and people who elected, retained, or reacquired Philippine citizenship.
- At least one accepted valid ID, such as PhilID/ePhilID or another government ID accepted by DFA.
- For women choosing their spouse's surname: PSA-issued Certificate of Marriage or Report of Marriage.

**Bring the original documents and ID, with one photocopy for submission. Personal appearance is required.** Supporting records depend on citizenship and personal circumstances. [1]

## Published domestic fees

DFA's **2025 charter** publishes these charges. [1]

| Charge | Philippine pesos |
| --- | ---: |
| Regular passport application | ₱950 |
| Expedited passport application | ₱1,200 |
| Additional convenience fee | ₱50 |

## Application stages

1. Book through the official passport appointment system and complete the payment step.
2. Attend your selected consular office. Present your printed application and ID for appointment verification.
3. Submit the required records for document evaluation.
4. Complete passport enrollment: information encoding, document scanning, photograph, and biometrics.
5. Follow the release instructions and check the passport's details when you receive it. [2]

DFA states that **appointment booking is free**. The application fees above are separate from obtaining an appointment. [3]

[Book a DFA passport appointment](https://passport.gov.ph/appointment/)

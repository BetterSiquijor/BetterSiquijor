# DSWD crisis assistance (AICS)

The **Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations** program supports people and families facing a crisis. DSWD's 2025 charter covers indigent, marginalized, vulnerable, disadvantaged, and financially incapacitated applicants, subject to a social worker's assessment. Assistance can include medical, funeral, food, transportation, psychosocial support, and referrals. [1]

## Prepare your documents

Bring your **original valid government-issued ID and two photocopies**, as specified in the 2025 charter. Examples include National ID, UMID, SSS/GSIS, PhilHealth, PRC, and OWWA/OFW ID. [1]

Prepare the records relevant to your request. DSWD's AICS FAQ lists these examples; documents are submitted as originals or certified true copies. [2]

| Request | Supporting records |
| --- | --- |
| Hospital bill | Medical certificate, clinical abstract, discharge summary, or other accepted medical record; hospital bill or statement of account. For an outstanding obligation, certificate of balance or promissory note. |
| Medicines | Medical record and prescription or treatment protocol; price quotation as applicable. |
| Laboratory examination | Doctor's order or laboratory request and official price quotation. |
| Funeral expenses | Death certificate or accepted death certification; funeral contract or statement of account. Outstanding obligations may require a balance certificate or promissory note. |
| Transportation | Documents establishing the circumstance, such as a medical certificate, death certificate, police certification, court order, or subpoena, depending on the request. |

## Application process

1. Present the documents for screening through DSWD's crisis-intervention service. The charter identifies CIS, SWAD, satellite offices, and Malasakit Centers as delivery channels. [1]
2. Attend the social worker's interview and assessment. The worker evaluates the actual need, documentary evidence, eligibility, appropriate service, and any financial assistance recommended for approval. [3]
3. After approval, follow the release instructions for the assistance or referral. [3]

**Assistance is assessed individually.** The amount depends on actual need, supporting records, and program funds; an application does not establish a fixed entitlement. [3]

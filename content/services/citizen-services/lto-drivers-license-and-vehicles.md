## Online portal and driver’s education

Before visiting the district office, set up your account on LTO’s official online portal:

- **LTMS Online Portal:** [portal.lto.gov.ph](https://portal.lto.gov.ph) — used to manage driver’s license applications, renewals, and transaction records. [2]
- **Comprehensive Driver’s Education (CDE) Online Validation Exam:** [portal.lto.gov.ph CDE Exam](https://portal.lto.gov.ph/ords/f?p=PUBLIC_PORTAL:ONLINE_VALIDATION_EXAM) and [LTO CDE Program overview](https://lto.gov.ph/comprehensive-drivers-education-program/). [3]

## Official requirements and Citizen’s Charter

LTO publishes its full, binding documentary checklists and fee tables inside its official Citizen’s Charter PDFs rather than as plain web pages: [4–5]

- **Student-Driver’s Permit (Walk-in & Portal)** and **Driver’s License Renewal** (plain/advance renewal and renewal with examination): see the [LTO 2026 Citizen’s Charter (1st Edition, PDF)](https://lto.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Citizens-Charter-2026-1st-Edition-2.pdf) and the [LTO Driver’s License page](https://lto.gov.ph/drivers-license/). [4]
- **Motor Vehicle / Motorcycle Registration** (initial registration and annual renewal): see the [LTO Motor Vehicle Citizen’s Charter (PDF)](https://lto.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/MV-CC-2025.pdf) and the [LTO Motor Vehicle page](https://lto.gov.ph/motor-vehicle/). [5]

Confirm local walk-in cut-off times and medical/emission clinic availability directly with LTO Siquijor District Office before travelling.

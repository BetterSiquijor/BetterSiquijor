# Senior Citizen, PWD, and Solo Parent IDs

Apply for a Senior Citizen ID, Person with Disability (PWD) ID, or Solo Parent ID through your municipal hall in Siquijor, and verify your registration through national portals.

## Senior Citizen ID (OSCA)

Under **Republic Act No. 9994** (_Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010_), Filipino citizens aged **60 years and older** are eligible for a national Senior Citizen ID. [1]

- **Where to apply:** The **Office for Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA)** at your municipal hall, assisted by the Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO). [1]
- **Cost and validity:** Issued **free of charge** and valid anywhere in the Philippines. [1]
- **National digital registration:** You can also register online via the **National Commission of Senior Citizens (NCSC)** Senior Citizens Data Form ([ncsc.gov.ph/seniorcitizensdataform](https://www.ncsc.gov.ph/seniorcitizensdataform)) and check the National Senior Citizens Digital ID portal ([ncsc.gov.ph/ncsid](https://www.ncsc.gov.ph/ncsid)). [2]
- **Documents to confirm locally:** Exact photo counts or local barangay-certification formats vary by municipality; confirm the local checklist with your municipal OSCA or MSWDO before visiting. See [municipal office contacts](/government/departments/office-directory).

## Person with Disability (PWD) ID

Under **RA 7277** (as amended by **RA 9442** and **RA 10754**) and **NCDA Administrative Order No. 001, s. 2021**, eligible residents with permanent disabilities—including people with cancer (RA 11215) and rare diseases (RA 10747)—apply through their municipal **MSWDO** or **Persons with Disabilities Affairs Office (PDAO)**. [3]

### Requirements for new applicants [3]

1. **Accomplished PWD ID Application Form (`PWDID-AF`)**, available from the MSWDO, PDAO, DOH, or NCDA.
2. **Confirmation of disability:**
   - **Apparent disability** (physically visible): A physician’s Certificate of Disability is **not required**; the issuing officer may assess the applicant directly (or refer to the Municipal Health Office if in doubt).
   - **Non-apparent disability** (e.g., deaf/hard of hearing, intellectual, learning, mental, psychosocial, non-apparent visual or speech impairment, cancer, or rare disease): A **Certificate of Disability** or medical certificate from an appropriate specialist or physician at a government health office or recognized medical institution.
3. **Two (2) recent 1×1 ID photos** with your name and signature or thumbmark on the back.
4. **One (1) valid government ID** (or a Barangay Certificate if unavailable; for minors, a School ID or photocopy of the Birth Certificate). Guardians or authorized representatives must bring proof of guardianship or a notarized authorization letter.
5. **Proof of residence** (Voter’s ID, Barangay Certificate, or utility bill showing your name and address).

- **Renewal or lost ID:** Submit a new `PWDID-AF` (mark Renewal) with the expired ID, or an **Affidavit of Loss** plus two 1×1 photos if lost. [3]
- **National verification:** Municipal encoders enter approved IDs into the **DOH Philippine Registry for Persons with Disability (PRPWD)** at [pwd.doh.gov.ph](https://pwd.doh.gov.ph/home.php), which also supports online ID verification. [4]

## Solo Parent ID (SPIC)

Under **Republic Act No. 11861** (_Expanded Solo Parents’ Welfare Act_), solo parents caring for unmarried, non-working dependent children **22 years old and below** (including expanded categories such as spouses of OFWs away for at least one year, legal guardians/foster parents, and pregnant women without partners) apply at their municipal **Solo Parent Office (SPO) / MSWDO**. [5–7]

### Core documentary requirements [7]

1. **Barangay certification** confirming solo parent status.
2. **Birth certificates** of dependent children.
3. **Proof of income** (for income-based benefits such as the 10% discount and VAT exemption for those earning below ₱250,000/year, or the ₱1,000 monthly LGU subsidy for minimum-wage earners and below). [5, 7]
4. **Supporting documents based on status** (e.g., death certificate of spouse, court documents for legal separation, or proof of abandonment/absence). [7]

- **Processing and validity:** DSWD states that the Solo Parent ID (SPIC) is **valid for one (1) year** and issued **within seven (7) days** from receipt of complete requirements. [6]

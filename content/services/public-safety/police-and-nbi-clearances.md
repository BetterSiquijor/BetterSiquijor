# Police clearance and NBI clearance

Apply online for a **National Police Clearance** at a local Siquijor police station, or prepare for an **NBI Clearance** appointment at the nearest NBI office in Dumaguete City.

## National Police Clearance System (NPCS)

The Philippine National Police processes national police clearances through [pnpclearance.ph](https://pnpclearance.ph/). [1]

### How to apply [1–2]

1. **Register or sign in** at [pnpclearance.ph](https://pnpclearance.ph/register) and complete your applicant profile.
2. **Schedule an appointment** and select your preferred police station. See [police stations in Siquijor](/services/public-safety/police-stations) for local station contacts.
3. **Pay online** via the redirected **Landbank ePayment Portal** (supports online channels as well as GCash/PayMaya/7-Eleven/Bayad Center via Cash Payment option). Save your **Payment Confirmation Slip or Electronic Official Receipt**.
4. **Appear at the selected police station** on your appointment date with:
   - **One (1) valid government-issued ID**
   - **Proof of payment / Payment Confirmation**
   - **NPCS Appointment Reference Number**
   - Complete on-site biometrics, photo, and signature capture.

### Renewals and First-Time Job Seekers [1–2]

- **Non-appearance renewal:** Applicants who completed an in-person National Police Clearance within the **last three (3) years** may use the non-appearance renewal option in the same account. [1]
- **First-Time Job Seekers (RA 11261):** Bring your **First-Time Job Seeker (FTJS) Certification** and **one (1) valid ID**. The free clearance can be availed once; first-time applicants must appear in person. [2]

**NPCS One Stop Shop support:** [(02) 8723-0401](tel:0287230401) local 7663 · [+63 927 964 6657](tel:+639279646657) (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM). [1]

### Online appointment, fees, and renewal [5–6]

- **Official portal:** [clearance.nbi.gov.ph](https://clearance.nbi.gov.ph/) (new registration, appointment scheduling, First-Time Jobseekers under RA 11261, and online verification). [5]
- **Online renewal with delivery:** Clearances issued **16 October 2016 onwards** can be renewed online with door-to-door delivery through [clearance.nbi.gov.ph](https://clearance.nbi.gov.ph/) without crossing to Dumaguete. [5]
- **Published fee:** Basic clearance fee is **₱130**, plus e-payment service charge (typically **₱25–₱30**) via GCash, Maya, 7-Eleven, Bayad Center, or online banking. Paid transactions carry a 15-day grace period. [6]
- **What to bring to the branch:** Printed or digital Reference Number / QR code, proof of payment, and **two (2) original, unexpired valid government-issued IDs** (e.g., Passport, Driver’s License, National ID, UMID, Postal ID, or PSA Birth Certificate). First-Time Job Seekers bring an original Barangay Certificate and signed Oath of Undertaking. [6]
- **“HIT” verification:** Records flagged with a “HIT” typically take **5 to 10 working days** for manual verification at no extra fee. [6]

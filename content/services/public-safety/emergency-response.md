# Emergency and disaster assistance

Get urgent help in Siquijor or contact provincial and municipal disaster, fire, police, coast guard, health, road, and utility response lines.

**For immediate emergencies, call [911](tel:911).** Tell the operator your location, municipality, barangay, and what happened. [1–2]

## Sources and reference dates

Telephone lines above have not been call-tested by BetterSiquijor. For urgent fire, medical, or rescue response, call **911** rather than waiting for an email or social-media reply. Municipal fire, RHU, MDRRMO, DPWH, and Coast Guard sub-station lines integrated on **18 November 2026** come from undated user-supplied PDRRMO hotline files retained under `data/hotlines/`, as BFP and Coast Guard national web portals blocked automated review; inclusion in the supplied files does not constitute live operational verification.

Discrepancies preserved without silent editing:

- **PDRRMO**: `0953 209 9165` appears in `siquijorprovince.com`, PDRRMO Facebook, the emergency assistance list, and the hotline directory header (`09532099165`), whereas `0933-209-9165` appears in the `Province of Siquijor` section of the hotline directory; `pdrrmosiquijor07@gmail.com` appears on PDRRMO Facebook, whereas the hotline header prints `pdrmosiquijor07@gmail.com` (single `r`) alongside `sptpdrrmosiquijor@gmail.com`.
- **Bureau of Fire Protection**: `035-480-9051` (hotline directory provincial row) differs from `035 480 9123` / `0926 410 2433` (assistance contacts list) and `035-480-0054` / `0926-410-2433` / `0935-480-9123` (Municipality of Siquijor row).
- **Siquijor Provincial Hospital vs. RHU**: `0935 924 7077` appears under Provincial Hospital in the assistance list and under Municipality of Siquijor RHU in the hotline directory.
- **San Juan duplicate block & police differences**: All five `Municipality of San Juan` rows in the hotline directory duplicate `Municipality of Siquijor`; Enrique Villanueva police differs (`0996-835-1602` vs. `0906 835 1602` on station Facebook) and Siquijor police differs (`0997-558-9114` vs. `0977 180 3999` on the undated station site).

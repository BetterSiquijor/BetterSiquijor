# Marriage licenses, civil registry, and record corrections

Prepare for a marriage license application, request PSA marriage, death, or CENOMAR certificates, or file an administrative correction for clerical errors at your Municipal Civil Registrar in Siquijor.

## Applying for a marriage license

Under **Executive Order No. 209** (_The Family Code of the Philippines_, Articles 9–20), a marriage license is issued by the **Local Civil Registrar (LCR)** of the municipality where **either contracting party habitually resides**. [1]

### Core statutory requirements [1]

- **Separate sworn applications** filed by each party stating full name, place and date of birth, civil status, residence, citizenship, degree of relationship, and parents’ details (Art. 11).
- **Original or duly attested Birth or Baptismal Certificate** (Art. 12). If previously married, bring the deceased spouse’s **Death Certificate** or the **judicial decree** of annulment, declaration of nullity, or absolute divorce (Art. 13).
- **Ages 18 to 21 — Parental Consent (Art. 14):** Written consent of the father, mother, surviving parent, or guardian (given in person before the LCR or via notarized affidavit with two witnesses), plus a **marriage counseling certificate** (Art. 16).
- **Ages 21 to 25 — Parental Advice (Art. 15):** Sworn statement that parental advice was sought together with the written advice, plus a **marriage counseling certificate** (Art. 16). If parental advice is unfavorable or not obtained, the license is issued only after three (3) months following publication.
- **10-day posting period (Art. 17):** The LCR posts notice of the application for **10 consecutive days** outside the office before issuing the license.
- **Validity and fees (Arts. 19–20):** A marriage license is valid anywhere in the Philippines for **120 days** from issue. Prescribed local fees apply, except for **indigent parties**, who are exempt upon oath/affidavit.

Confirm any additional municipal checklists (such as CENOMAR or pre-marriage seminar schedules) with your Municipal Civil Registrar via the [office directory](/government/departments/office-directory).

## Requesting PSA marriage, death, and CENOMAR certificates

For PSA-issued security paper copies, prepare the following details listed on **PSA’s official portals** [2–4]:

- **Marriage Certificate:** Complete names of husband and wife, date and place of marriage, requester’s name and address, number of copies, and purpose. [2]
- **Death Certificate:** Complete name of deceased, date and place of death, requester’s name and address, number of copies, and purpose. [3]
- **CENOMAR (Certificate of No Marriage):** Complete name of the person, father’s complete name, mother’s complete maiden name, date and place of birth, requester’s name and address, number of copies, and purpose. [4]

**Official online ordering channels** linked from [psa.gov.ph](https://psa.gov.ph/civilregistration):

- [PSA Serbilis](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/)
- [PSA Helpline](https://psahelpline.ph/)

For PSA birth certificates and delivery fee details, see [Get a PSA certificate](/services/civil-documents/birth-certificates).

## Correcting clerical errors or changing a first name (RA 9048 & RA 10172)

Under **RA 9048** (as amended by **RA 10172**), your **Municipal Civil Registrar** can administratively correct—**without a court order**—[5–6]:

- Clerical or typographical errors in civil registry entries;
- Change of **first name or nickname**;
- Clerical errors in the **day and month of birth** or **sex** of a person.

_Exclusions:_ Changes involving **nationality, age (year of birth), or civil status** are not covered and require judicial proceedings. [5–6]

### Supporting documents for administrative petitions [6]

1. **Certified true copy** of the certificate or registry book page containing the error.
2. At least **two (2) public or private documents** showing the correct entry.
3. **For day/month of birth or sex corrections:** Earliest school records, medical records, or baptismal/religious documents; for sex corrections, a **certification from an accredited government physician** confirming the petitioner has not undergone sex change or transplant.
4. **Law enforcement clearance** certifying no pending case or criminal record.
5. **Newspaper publication:** Petitions for change of first name/nickname, day/month of birth, or sex must be published **once a week for two (2) consecutive weeks** in a newspaper of general circulation.

Petitions are filed in person in **three (3) copies** at the Local Civil Registry Office where the record is kept (or via migrant petition where applicable). Indigent petitioners are exempt from filing fees. [5–6]

For **late / delayed registration**, check your Municipal Civil Registrar’s documentary checklist directly, as municipal LCR checklists in Siquijor are not published online.

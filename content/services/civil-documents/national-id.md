# National ID information

National service: official information and access to the Digital National ID.

## Official National ID resources

[Open the Digital National ID website](https://national-id.gov.ph/) or [read PSA PhilSys information](https://philsys.gov.ph/).

Use the official PSA resources to understand the service you need. This portal does not collect identity documents, issue identity cards, or operate a National ID registration center.

We have not verified the current schedule of registration centers in Siquijor. Confirm a center and its requirements through PSA before travelling.

# National ID registration and Digital National ID

Register for your National ID or download a Digital National ID if you are already registered. Find the steps and fee information below.

## If you are already registered with PhilSys

To access your Digital National ID: [1]

1. Use a computer or smartphone with internet and a camera for the facial verification step.
2. Open the official Digital National ID service at `national-id.gov.ph`.
3. Choose **Proceed** and review the consent for using identity information and the facial image to authenticate you.
4. Enter your first, middle, and last names and date of birth, then continue.
5. Start the **liveness** check and follow the facial-verification instructions.
6. After successful verification, display your National ID and use the download option to retain a personal copy.

You must already be registered with PhilSys to retrieve a Digital National ID. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete identity verification.

## Fees and timing

**Assisted Digital National ID generation is free.** The **2025 PSA charter** sets a **seven-minute** service standard, subject to internet connectivity. Allow time for queuing and identity verification. First-time registration and plastic-card delivery follow separate processes. [2]

## If you have never registered

PSA’s registration notice says its old online Step 1 registration service is disabled and applicants may register in person at a PhilSys registration center with supporting documents. [3] A Digital National ID download cannot substitute for that registration.

Bring your supporting identity documents to a PhilSys registration center. Contact PSA before travelling to check the center’s schedule and documents for your circumstances.

## Access your Digital National ID

[Digital National ID access](https://national-id.gov.ph/) is the official retrieval service. BetterSiquijor does not ask for your date of birth, take your photo, or run the biometric check.

# Birth, marriage, and death certificates

National service: official PSA channels for civil registry documents.

## Start from the Philippine Statistics Authority

[PSA birth certificate information](https://psa.gov.ph/birth-certificate) explains the PSA’s document channels. The PSA website links to [PSA Serbilis](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/) as an online application option.

## Find the right document channel

1. Read the PSA information for the document you need.
2. Use the official channel linked by PSA and review its requirements, delivery options, and charges before submitting.
3. Keep any application reference and follow up through the service provider’s official channel.

This is a national service available to Siquijor residents. For corrections or a locally registered record, consult the relevant local civil registrar. We have not verified municipal processing times or fees.

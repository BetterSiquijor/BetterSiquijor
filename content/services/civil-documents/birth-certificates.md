# Birth, marriage, and death certificates

Request a PSA birth, marriage, or death certificate. Find the details to prepare, application steps, delivery options, and fees below.

## Which information should you prepare?

Have the following record details ready before starting your request. Identity and authorization checks apply when the certificate is released.

### Birth certificate

- Child’s full name, including middle name.
- Father’s full name and mother’s full maiden name.
- Birth date and municipality/city and province of birth.
- Whether registration was late and, if so, the year it was registered.
- Requester’s full name, address, and relationship to the child.
- Number of copies and the reason for the request. [1]

### Marriage certificate

Prepare both spouses’ full names, the marriage date and place, your own full name and address, the quantity needed, and the request’s purpose. [2]

### Death certificate

Prepare the deceased person’s full name, date and place of death, your own complete name and address, the quantity needed, and the request’s purpose. The national page additionally lists the place of marriage among its death-request fields; use the actual issuing channel’s form to establish whether that field applies. [3]

## How to request a certificate

1. Identify the certificate and gather its record details above.
2. Choose an **in-person PSA outlet request** or an **online delivery request**. PSA’s birth-certificate page says an appointment is required for walk-in requests. Its linked appointment service is `appointment.psa.gov.ph`. PSA identifies **PSA Serbilis** and **PSAHelpline** as online channels. [1]
3. Give your real requester and record details to the chosen official channel. Birth records have restricted release; the PSA page lists the record owner, authorized persons, and specified relatives or legal representatives among those who may request them. [1]
4. Follow that channel’s identity/authorization checks and retain its reference or receipt. For an outlet transaction, follow the release instruction on your receipt rather than assume same-day release.

For online submission, PSA’s listed channels are [PSA Serbilis](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/) and [PSAHelpline](https://psahelpline.ph/).

## PSA Serbilis fees for Philippine delivery and related channels

The provider’s published FAQ lists these charges. The figures are for **PSA Serbilis**, not a universal PSA outlet or PSAHelpline price. The FAQ is undated; the figures below were checked on **17 November 2026**. [4]

| Receiving option | Birth, marriage, or death | CENOMAR or CENODEATH |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First copy: Philippine door delivery, LBC pickup, or embassy delivery | ₱330 | ₱430 |
| Each additional copy under its published tiered-pricing example | ₱280 | ₱380 |
| Government-agency E-Copy | ₱300 | ₱360 |
| Viewable Online | ₱130 | ₱185 |

**Viewable Online** is paid at a PSA CRS outlet for identity verification and an access code. Its optional **DocPrint** paper copy is **₱80 per copy**, requested within the online document’s **60-day** validity. Government-agency E-Copy and Viewable Online are separate receiving options; they are not the same as paper courier delivery. [4]

## Delivery to Siquijor

Siquijor falls under the provider’s **Other Provinces** destination category. Its delivery page estimates **6–8 working days** for that category, with **5–7 additional days** outside the courier’s standard service areas. It asks customers to allow **10–15 additional working days** for requests requiring manual PSA searching. Flight unavailability or cancellation can alter the schedule. These are provider estimates, not a fixed Siquijor island delivery guarantee. [5]

The request’s receiving option and address determine the total shown before payment. A certificate order is a different transaction from a correction, late registration, or marriage-license application at the relevant civil registrar.

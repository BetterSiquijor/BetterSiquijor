# PhilHealth registration and member services

Register with PhilHealth, access your Member Data Record, or manage your membership online. Find the steps and Siquijor office contact below.

## Choose the transaction that matches your situation

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Employed, no PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) yet | Complete two PMRF copies and submit them to your employer’s HR unit; await the Member Data Record (MDR) and ID through the employer. [1] |
| Newly hired, already has a PIN | Give the existing PIN to the employer for employee reporting. [1] |
| Applying under PhilHealth’s informal-economy procedure | Submit two completed PMRF copies to a Local Health Insurance Office or PhilHealth Express and await the MDR and ID. The published procedure also covers payment using the assigned number. [2] |
| Already registered and needs records online | Create or use a Member Portal account. [3–4] |

Use your existing PIN if you already have one. Choose the procedure that matches your membership category.

## Existing-member portal: what to prepare

The account-creation screen asks for a **PIN, names, birth date, sex, email address, mobile number, and a chosen password**. It provides a mononym option. The password requirements shown are **8–32 characters**, with a digit, upper- and lowercase letters, and a special character. Complete its CAPTCHA and account-creation action. [4]

For later login, the portal requests the PIN, password, and CAPTCHA. A password-reset request asks for the **PIN and email address** and sends a reset link. [5]

PhilHealth lists online access to contribution records and the MDR, premium payment, and YAKAP clinic selection among member services. Those functions do not themselves determine a particular patient’s benefit entitlement. [3]

## Manage your membership

Use the agency’s [Member Portal](https://memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/) for an online transaction or the proper employer/PhilHealth office for registration. Submit membership details and payments directly to PhilHealth or your employer, as applicable.

# PhilHealth member services

National service: membership records, contributions, and benefit information.

## Start with PhilHealth

[Open PhilHealth online services](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/services/). The official services page links to the Member Portal and benefit information. Members can access their records, contributions, and Member Data Record through the official portal.

## Before using an online service

- Open the member service from the official PhilHealth website.
- Follow the instructions for the transaction you need.
- Confirm coverage or facility questions with PhilHealth or your healthcare provider.

This link serves residents nationwide, including Siquijor. It is not a provincial application form. We do not publish unverified benefit amounts or guarantee eligibility.

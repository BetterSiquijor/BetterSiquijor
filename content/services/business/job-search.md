# Find work through PhilJobNet and PESO

**PhilJobNet** is the Department of Labor and Employment’s public job-matching service. Jobseekers can register without a fee, search and save opportunities, see job matches with employer contacts, and respond to invitations from accredited employers.

## Create a jobseeker account

1. Choose **Jobseeker/applicant** on the official PhilJobNet sign-up form.
2. Enter your full name, a valid email address, and a password meeting the form’s requirements.
3. Open the activation email and activate the account. Check the spam folder if it is not in the inbox.
4. Complete the jobseeker profile and job preferences so the system can match relevant opportunities.

The sign-up page requires a password of at least **eight characters**, including a digit, an uppercase character, and a permitted special character. The Bureau of Local Employment’s published client requirements describe jobseekers as Filipino citizens with a Philippine residential address who are **18 or older**. This is the service’s published account-eligibility guidance, not a statement about the legal minimum age for every form of work.

## Search, compare, and apply

- Search or browse job opportunities and save relevant listings.
- Review the employer, work location, qualifications, and application instructions for each vacancy.
- Use the system’s matching results and the employer contact information attached to them.
- Submit an application through the system where supported, or respond to an employer’s invitation using the posting’s instructions.
- Keep the profile and preferences accurate so later matches use the right information.

The platform accredits employers before allowing them to post vacancies. An employer’s accreditation is a platform status; the applicant must still read the individual job’s terms and complete the employer’s selection process.

## Assisted registration through PESO

A jobseeker may also register through the nearest **Public Employment Service Office (PESO)**. The PESO records the jobseeker profile in the **PESO Employment Information System (PEIS)**, which contributes to the National Skills Registry. This is the assisted route for someone who needs help registering or using the online service.

Ask your municipality’s PESO or municipal government about employment-registration assistance. The [local office directory](/government/departments/office-directory) lists municipal office addresses and contacts for all six towns.

## Service cost and account handling

PhilJobNet account creation and its public job-matching services are **free**. BetterSiquijor does not collect job applications, passwords, identity documents, or placement payments. Account activation and job applications are handled by PhilJobNet and the responsible employer or PESO.

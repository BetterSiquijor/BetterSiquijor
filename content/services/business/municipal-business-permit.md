# Municipal business permits and local offices

After registering a business name or entity, secure a **Business/Mayor’s Permit** from the municipality where your business operates before starting operations. [1]

## How local permits fit with national registration

1. **Register your business identity**: sole proprietors register a business name with DTI ([see the DTI BNRS guide](/services/business/business-name-registration)); corporations and partnerships register with SEC via the [Philippine Business Hub](https://business.gov.ph/home). [1–2]
2. **Apply for a municipal Business/Mayor’s Permit**: DTI notes that a Business Name certificate alone does not authorize operations — you must obtain a Business/Mayor’s Permit from the city or municipality where the business operates. [1]
3. **Complete BIR tax registration**: register with [Revenue District Office 79](/services/business/tax-registration).

Under Republic Act No. 11032, the [Philippine Business Hub](https://business.gov.ph/home) provides national business-registration workflows (Helpdesk: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM except holidays, `PBHhelpdesk@dict.gov.ph`). [2] For DTI regional enquiries in Central Visayas, contact **DTI Region 7** at `R07@dti.gov.ph`. [3]

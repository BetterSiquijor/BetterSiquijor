# Register a sole-proprietor business name

A DTI business-name registration gives a sole proprietorship its registered business identity. It is separate from the Business/Mayor’s Permit issued by the municipality where the business operates and from [BIR tax registration](/services/business/tax-registration).

## Who can register

DTI’s BNRS guidance permits a Filipino applicant aged **18 or older** to register a business name. A business using a name other than the owner’s true name must register that name. Foreign nationals and recognized refugees or stateless persons follow DTI’s additional documentary process.

## Prepare the application information

Have the owner’s name, an active email address, business and residence addresses, a proposed business name, and the description of the business activity ready. Choose the territorial scope before submitting. A representative needs a valid ID and an authorization letter signed by the owner, together with the applicable registration requirements.

## Registration steps

1. In the official BNRS **New Registration** service, accept the terms and enter the owner’s information.
2. Select the territorial scope. Enter the proposed dominant business name and choose the business-name descriptor that fits the activity. Use **Check Name Availability**.
3. Save the generated reference code. Complete the business address, owner details, and remaining application fields.
4. Review the information and confirm the undertaking.
5. Pay using a payment channel offered by BNRS. DTI’s guide requires payment within **seven calendar days** of application; an unpaid application is treated as abandoned.
6. After payment confirmation, obtain the certificate. For a later download, use **Transaction Inquiry**, enter the reference code, receive the email verification code, and select the certificate in the downloadable contents.

The official transaction system is **bnrs.dti.gov.ph**. BetterSiquijor provides the guide; DTI receives the application and payment.

## Published registration charges

The following DTI charges appear in the Board of Investments’ **2026 Cost of Doing Business** publication. The total column is the registration fee plus the stated documentary stamp tax.

| Territorial scope | Registration fee | Documentary stamp tax | Calculated total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Barangay | ₱200 | ₱30 | ₱230 |
| City/municipality | ₱500 | ₱30 | ₱530 |
| Regional | ₱1,000 | ₱30 | ₱1,030 |
| National | ₱2,000 | ₱30 | ₱2,030 |

These are DTI business-name charges, not municipal business-permit or BIR transaction charges. Territorial scope concerns where the registered business name may be used for business establishments; DTI says it is not the geographical limit for transacting business. A business-name registration is valid for **five years** from registration.

## After receiving the certificate

Keep the registration certificate and transaction reference. Obtain the Business/Mayor’s Permit from the municipality where the business is located and complete BIR registration. A DTI certificate alone does not authorize business operations.

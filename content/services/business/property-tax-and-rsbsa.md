# Real property tax (Amilyar) and RSBSA agriculture registration

Check statutory due dates for paying Real Property Tax at your Municipal Treasurer’s Office, and see how Siquijor farmers and fisherfolk register in the Department of Agriculture’s RSBSA registry.

## Real Property Tax (Amilyar)

Under **Republic Act No. 7160** (_Local Government Code of 1991_), the **Municipal Treasurer** (or a deputized, bonded Barangay Treasurer) collects basic Real Property Tax (RPT) and the additional Special Education Fund (SEF) tax. [1]

### Statutory payment schedule (RA 7160, Sec. 250) [1]

Property owners may pay annual RPT and SEF tax **without interest in four (4) equal quarterly installments**:

| Quarter         | Statutory due date            |
| --------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **1st Quarter** | On or before **31 March**     |
| **2nd Quarter** | On or before **30 June**      |
| **3rd Quarter** | On or before **30 September** |
| **4th Quarter** | On or before **31 December**  |

Payments are applied first to prior years’ delinquencies, interest, and penalties before current-year taxes. [1]

### Discounts and late-payment interest [1]

- **Prompt / advance payment discount (Sec. 251):** The local sanggunian may grant a discount of **up to 20%** of the annual tax due for advance or prompt payment.
- **Delinquency interest (Sec. 255):** Unpaid taxes after the due date incur **2% interest per month** on the unpaid amount (up to a maximum of **36 months**).

_Local verification note:_ The Bureau of Local Government Finance’s CY 2026 RPT notice index does not list a published Siquijor ordinance notice. [2] Confirm the exact prompt-payment discount rate, assessment notice, and accepted payment methods directly with your **Municipal Treasurer’s Office** or **Municipal Assessor’s Office** via the [office directory](/government/departments/office-directory).

## RSBSA (Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture)

The **Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA)** is the Department of Agriculture’s official registry of farmers, farmworkers, fisherfolk, and agri-youth used to verify eligibility for government agricultural support programs. [3]

### How to register or update your record [3–4]

1. **Visit your Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)** at your municipal hall in Siquijor.
2. **Complete the official RSBSA Enrollment Form** (available at the MAO or downloadable from [da.gov.ph/rsbsa-registration-and-updating](https://www.da.gov.ph/rsbsa-registration-and-updating/)). [3]
3. **Confirm supporting documents at the MAO** (such as a valid ID, ID photo, and proof of farming/fishing activity or land tenure) before submission; the MAO encodes verified forms into DA’s RSBSA system. [3]
4. **Verify your registration online:** Check whether your name is active in the registry using the official **DA RSBSA Finder** at [finder-rsbsa.da.gov.ph](https://finder-rsbsa.da.gov.ph/) (search by full name, sex, and date of birth). [4]

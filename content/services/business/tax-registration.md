# Register a business with the BIR

BIR registration records the taxpayer and business for tax administration. It is separate from the DTI business-name certificate and the municipality’s Business/Mayor’s Permit. The BIR identifies three registration routes: the **Online Registration and Update System (ORUS)**, email submission through **NewBizReg**, or the responsible Revenue District Office’s new-business counter.

## Prepare the individual-business documents

The BIR’s business-registration guide lists the following for sole proprietors and professionals:

- Accomplished **BIR Form 1901**.
- Government identification showing the applicant’s name, address, and birthdate; include proof of residence if the ID has no address.
- A valid PRC ID and address-bearing government ID or address proof for a PRC-regulated profession.
- DTI certificate when a business name is used.
- For a representative: a Special Power of Attorney and the representative’s government ID.
- For printing the taxpayer’s own invoices: a clear invoice sample and accomplished **Form 1906**. The guide also provides the alternative of buying BIR Printed Invoices from the RDO.

Corporations and partnerships use **Form 1903** and their applicable SEC registration documents. The correct taxpayer type matters; the individual-business checklist is not a corporate checklist.

## ORUS route

1. Create an ORUS account using the taxpayer’s permanent email address, or sign in to an existing account.
2. Select **New Registration** and the applicable individual or non-individual registration option.
3. Complete the registration details and submit the documents requested for that taxpayer type.
4. Follow the application’s assessment and registration-document instructions.

BIR’s account-setup guide specifies that an existing taxpayer’s ORUS account uses the permanent email already recorded with the BIR. The account is created in the official ORUS system, not on BetterSiquijor.

## NewBizReg email route

1. Scan the applicable documents as PDF files. BIR limits the combined attachments to **4 MB per email**.
2. Complete the tax-type questionnaire and include it with the application documents.
3. Pay the **₱30 loose documentary stamp tax**. An applicant with an existing TIN may use the listed BIR payment channels; an applicant without a TIN follows the RDO’s emailed payment instructions or pays at the new-business counter when collecting the certificate.
4. Select the business location in NewBizReg to identify the responsible RDO, then email the completed application and attachments to that office.
5. Keep the RDO’s acknowledgment. The portal states **three working days from acknowledgment of complete documentary requirements** for manual processing, followed by an email arranging certificate collection and applicable invoice/printing steps.

The published processing period starts with acknowledgment of **complete** documents, not merely sending an email. The RDO’s instructions determine the actual transaction outcome.

## Charges and Siquijor district

The BIR stopped collecting the **₱500 Annual Registration Fee** effective **22 January 2024**, under RMC 14-2024. It should not be added to the NewBizReg steps above. The published **₱30 documentary stamp tax** remains a separate item; invoice printing or purchase is a separate transaction.

Siquijor is included in **Revenue District Office 79 — Negros Oriental and Siquijor**. BIR’s 2026 FOI directory lists the district office at **BIR Building, Agapito Valencia Drive, Barangay Taclobo, Dumaguete City, Negros Oriental**. The same directory publishes **(035) 226-3697** as the RDO’s FOI contact telephone. Use this number for an FOI enquiry; ask the RDO for the registration counter handling your transaction.

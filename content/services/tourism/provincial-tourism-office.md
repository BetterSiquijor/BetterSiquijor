### DOT Online Accreditation (for tourism businesses & guides)

Accommodation establishments (hotels, resorts, homestays), travel/tour operators, tourist transport operators, ecotourism facilities, and tour guides apply for or renew **DOT Accreditation** online through the official **DOT Online Accreditation Portal**: [4]

- Portal login & registration: [accreditation.tourism.gov.ph](https://accreditation.tourism.gov.ph/login)
- Program overview: [tourism.gov.ph/dot/online-accreditations](https://www.tourism.gov.ph/dot/online-accreditations/)

## Official visitor guidelines in Siquijor

Published by the Siquijor Provincial Tourism Office: [5]

- **Zero-waste province:** Single-use plastic is strictly prohibited. Bring a reusable water tumbler and segregate waste properly.
- **No smoking in public places:** Smoking is prohibited in public areas, especially on beaches.
- **Respectful attire:** Wear appropriate clothing when visiting churches, banks, public markets, and town centers.
- **Road safety:** Follow local traffic rules when renting or riding motorbikes and tricycles.

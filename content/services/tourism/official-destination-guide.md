# Official Siquijor destination guide

National tourism information: the Department of Tourism’s Siquijor guide.

## Department of Tourism guide

[Read the official DOT Siquijor destination page](https://www.tourism.gov.ph/destination/nir/siquijor/).

The guide introduces Siquijor’s visitor destinations and places the province in the **Negros Island Region**. It is an external destination reference, not a live ferry timetable or booking page.

For current local arrangements, contact the [Provincial Tourism Office](/services/tourism/provincial-tourism-office). We do not publish unverified entrance fees, ferry schedules, or operating hours.

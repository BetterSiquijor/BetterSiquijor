# Siquijor destination guide

Discover Siquijor’s waterfalls, heritage, and local traditions. Explore places to visit and find help planning your trip.

## Explore the island

Siquijor is part of the **Negros Island Region**. The Department of Tourism calls it the **Healing Island**, a name associated with its cultural traditions. [1]

| Place or event | What to explore |
| --- | --- |
| Cambugahay Falls | A three-tier waterfall with turquoise pools and cascades |
| Lazi Convent | A Bahay na Bato structure completed in 1891 and home to the Siquijor Heritage Museum |
| Balay Pahiuli | Local traditional rituals and massage practices |
| Healing Festival | A Holy Week celebration at Mount Bandilaan Nature Park, with traditional knowledge, herbs, crafts, and local products |

## Getting to Siquijor

A ferry route linking **Lazi, Tagbilaran City, and Plaridel in Misamis Occidental** began commercial service on **23 October 2026**, according to the Philippine Information Agency. [2] Check your operator’s sailing schedule and fare before booking.

## Help planning your visit

Contact the **Provincial Tourism Office: 0935 055 9866** for visitor information. [3] Have your travel dates, destination, transport questions, and any accessibility needs ready.

See [Tourism Office services](/services/tourism/provincial-tourism-office) for more ways to plan your trip.

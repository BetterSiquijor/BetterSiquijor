# Siquijor destination guide

Explore Siquijor’s waterfalls, heritage churches, marine sanctuaries, caves, mountain forest reserve, and annual festivals across all six municipalities.

Siquijor is part of the **Negros Island Region (NIR)** and is promoted by the Department of Tourism as the **Healing Island** for its traditional wellness heritage and natural sites. [1]

## Destinations by municipality

### Siquijor (Capital Town)

- **St. Francis of Assisi Church & Bell Tower** (Poblacion) — Built in 1774, the oldest church on the island, with a separate bell tower that historically served as a watchtower. [2, 6]
- **Mt. Bandilaan Nature Park / Siquijor Forest Reserve** (Cang-agong; extends into Lazi) — Established under **Proclamation No. 523, s. 1940** (212 hectares). Siquijor’s highest peak at **557 m**, featuring a 5-storey viewing tower, Way of the Cross trail, watershed forest, habitat of the endemic streak-breasted bulbul, and the venue for the Holy Week Healing Festival. [3, 8]
- **Cantabon Cave** (Cantabon) — 800-meter cave on the northeastern side of Mt. Bandilaan featuring crystal terraces and limestone formations. [1, 3]
- **Caticugan Marine Protected Area** (Caticugan) — Community-managed marine sanctuary for snorkeling, kayaking, paddle boating, and diving (corals, giant clams, sea turtles). [4]
- **Balay Pahauli** (Cantabon) & **San Roque Church** (Banban) — Stops on the Provincial Tourism Office’s Healing and Wellness Tour showcasing _tuob_ fumigation, herbal traditions, and _hilot_. [1, 5]
- **Secret Lagoon** (San Antonio) — Featured on the Provincial Tourism Office mountain tour. [3]

### Lazi

- **Cambugahay Falls** (Canclaran) — Three-tiered rainforest waterfall with turquoise swimming pools; Siquijor’s most visited attraction. [1–2]
- **San Isidro Labrador Parish Church and Convent** (Poblacion, Lazi) — Recognized by the **National Museum of the Philippines (2001)** as a **National Cultural Treasure** and listed on the **UNESCO World Heritage Tentative List** (_Baroque Churches of the Philippines Extension_). Built in the late 19th century (church 1884; convent completed 1891), featuring coral-stone architecture and one of the largest _conventos_ in the Visayas, home to the Siquijor Heritage Museum. [1–2, 6–7]
- **Centuries-Old Balete Tree** (Campalanas) — Ancient banyan tree estimated at nearly 500 years old with a natural cold spring and fish-spa pool at its base. [2]
- **Hapitanan** (Tignao) & **Señora River Trekking** — Viewpoint overlooking Crocodile Hill and a ~200-meter river trek listed on official tourism routes. [2, 5]

### Maria

- **Salagdoong Beach** (Olang) — White-sand cove 6 km northeast of Maria with a nearby islet. _(Note: The Provincial Tourism Office’s 2023 website states swimming was temporarily suspended pending DENR Forest Land Agreement approval; confirm current open status with the Provincial Tourism Office or LGU before visiting.)_ [2]
- **Kagusuan Beach** (Minalulan) — Quiet white-sand beach framed by coral boulders. [2–3]
- **Olang Marine Sanctuary** (Olang) — Second-largest marine protected area on the island and 2019 Isla de Fuego MPA Awards honoree. [4]
- **Our Lady of Divine Providence Parish** (Maria) — Listed heritage parish. [2]

### San Juan

- **Paliton Beach** (Paliton) — Palm-lined white-sand beach, marine protected area, and popular sunset spot. [2–3]
- **Tubod Marine Sanctuary** (Tubod) — Snorkeling and marine life sanctuary near Coco Grove Beach Resort, known for sea turtles and reefs. [4]
- **Lugnason Falls** (Napo) — Rainforest waterfall with aquamarine pools. [3]
- **Pitogo Cliff** (Tag-ibo) — Coastal rock formations, sunset spot, and marine-life viewing. [3]
- **Seashell Museum** (Tubod) & **St. Augustine Parish** (San Juan) — Marine shell exhibits and town parish. [2, 4]

### Larena

- **Larena Triad Viewpoint** (Nonoc) — Hilltop viewpoint overlooking Larena with views across Negros, Cebu, and Bohol. [2]
- **Siquijor Butterfly Garden** (Sitio Cabilao, Cang-apa) — Local butterfly conservation and breeding project. [3]
- **St. Vincent Ferrer Parish** (Larena) — Town parish. [2]

### Enrique Villanueva

- **Tulapos Marine Sanctuary and Freediving Site** (Tulapos) — Snorkeling and freediving sanctuary known for giant trevallies, barracudas, blacktip reef sharks, giant clams, and sea turtles. [4]
- **Holy Mountain of God Trek** (Cangmanki) — Hilltop pilgrimage trek with a 30-foot cross. [5]
- **Our Lady of Mount Carmel Parish** (Enrique Villanueva) — Town parish, alongside local bakery stops (_Lilibeth’s Pan Bisaya_ and _Elena’s Bakery_). [2]

## Best season for marine sanctuaries

The Provincial Tourism Office recommends planning snorkeling and dive stops by monsoon season: [4]

- **Northeast Monsoon (June–November):** Caticugan MPA (Siquijor) and Tulapos Marine Sanctuary (Enrique Villanueva).
- **Southwest Monsoon (December–May):** Olang Marine Sanctuary (Maria) and Tubod Marine Sanctuary (San Juan).

## Getting here and around

- **Sea travel:** See [Ports and sea travel](/services/tourism/ports-and-ferries) for Port of Siquijor, Port of Larena, and Port of Lazi profiles and PPA shipping-line contacts (including the Lazi–Tagbilaran–Plaridel ferry route launched 23 October 2026). [11]
- **Island transport:** Siquijor has no metered taxis or public buses. Visitors travel by **tricycle**, **jeepney/easyride** (fixed routes), **van for hire**, or **rented motorbike**. [9]
- **Visitor assistance & eco-rules:** See [Provincial Tourism Office & visitor guidelines](/services/tourism/provincial-tourism-office) for PTO contacts, DOT NIR details, and zero-waste rules.

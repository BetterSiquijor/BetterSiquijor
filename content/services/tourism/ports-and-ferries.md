# Ports and sea travel

Find Siquijor’s ports, managing port offices, and shipping-line contacts published by the **Philippine Ports Authority (PPA)**.

## Ports in Siquijor

| Port             | Location                          | Overview and routes listed by PPA                                                                                                     |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Port of Siquijor | Siquijor, Siquijor                | Primary gateway for RoRo and fastcraft ferries to and from Dumaguete; PPA schedule page also lists Tagbilaran [1–2]                   |
| Port of Larena   | North Poblacion, Larena, Siquijor | RoRo and fastcraft gateway; PPA schedule page lists Plaridel, Tagbilaran, and Samboan [1, 3]                                          |
| Port of Lazi     | Tigbawan, Lazi, Siquijor          | 65 m berth and 68-passenger terminal building; PIA reported a Lazi–Tagbilaran–Plaridel ferry route launched on 23 October 2026 [1, 4] |

## Airport note

An older undated Provincial Tourism Office page describes Siquijor Airport in Barangay Cang-alwang, Siquijor as closed for rehabilitation for commercial operations (and described Lazi Port as non-operational at that earlier time). [7]

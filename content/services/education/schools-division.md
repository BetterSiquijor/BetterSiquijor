# Basic education enrollment in Siquijor

Enroll a learner in basic education. Prepare the enrollment form and supporting documents, then submit them to the learner’s school.

[Find a school near you](/services/education/find-school).

## Who handles enrollment?

The learner’s school receives the enrollment documents. The Schools Division of Siquijor’s published office is **Helen, Larena, Siquijor**; its email is **deped.siquijor@deped.gov.ph** and published mobile is **0906 432 9162**. [1]

## How to enroll

DepEd’s **13 June 2025 Revised Basic Education Enrollment Policy** and its following-day public explainer describe these arrangements: [2–3]

1. Complete the **Basic Education Enrollment Form (BEEF)** and submit it with the applicable supporting documents to the school.
2. For a minor, the enrollment form is signed by a parent or legal guardian; an adult learner may submit their own enrollment.
3. The policy calls for the original or certified true copy of a PSA/NSO birth certificate, submitted **once across K–12**. Where it is unavailable, the published policy allows specified secondary documents, including a National ID, local certificate of live birth, barangay certification, or a parent-signed undertaking. The **2025 explainer** refers to **31 October** for temporary secondary-document acceptance; this guide does not present that as a new 2026 deadline.
4. Enrollment can be conducted in person or using the school’s designated remote submission arrangements, such as email, messaging, or a drop box.
5. For a transfer, school records are transmitted **school to school** rather than treated as a new parent-printed requirement.

## Fees and school-year dates

The **2025 DepEd explainer** states that voluntary fees are not collected during early registration and enrollment. This is not a claim that every school-related purchase or private-school tuition is free.

## Before you contact the school

Have the learner’s name, grade level, existing Learner Reference Number if applicable, and school ready when you ask about enrollment. Ask the school to identify its currently accepted submission method before sending personal documents.

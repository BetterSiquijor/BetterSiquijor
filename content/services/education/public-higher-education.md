# Public higher education in Siquijor

Explore courses at **Siquijor State College in Larena**. Compare programs in education, business, technology, maritime studies, and other fields.

[Find all colleges in Siquijor, campus contacts, and private college courses](/services/education/colleges).

## Institution and location

Siquijor State College is in **Larena, Siquijor**. Contact the college at **presoffice@siquijorstate.edu.ph** for help reaching the appropriate office.

## Courses and programs

Choose an academic area to explore the college’s program offerings. Ask the college about available intakes and admission requirements for your chosen course. [2]

Choose your course and specialization, then ask the college about application dates, entrance assessments, and the documents required for admission.

# Public higher education in Siquijor

Explore courses at **Siquijor State College in Larena**. Compare programs in engineering, education, business, technology, maritime studies, and other fields.

[Find all colleges in Siquijor, campus contacts, and private college courses](/services/education/colleges).

## Institution and location

Siquijor State College is in **Larena, Siquijor**. Contact the college at **presoffice@siquijorstate.edu.ph** for help reaching the appropriate office.

## Courses and programs

Choose an academic area to explore the college’s program offerings. Undergraduate programs follow the college’s **18 June 2025** official Facebook announcement across seven colleges [2]; graduate programs and basic-education tracks remain from the college’s undated website catalogue [3]. Ask the college about available intakes and admission requirements for your chosen course.

| Academic unit                         | Programs                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| College of Engineering                | Civil Engineering; Electrical Engineering; Mechanical Engineering (marked as new undergraduate offerings in June 2025) [2]                                                                                           |
| College of Arts and Sciences          | Filipino Language; English Language; Political Science; Development Communication [2]                                                                                                                                |
| College of Business and Management    | Business Administration bachelor’s with General Management, Financial Management, Marketing Management, or Human Resource Management; Hospitality Management; Tourism Management [2]; Public Management master’s [3] |
| College of Criminal Justice Education | Criminology bachelor’s program [2]                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| College of Technology                 | Information Technology; Industrial Technology with Automotive, Mechanical, Electrical, or Electronics Technology [2]; Industrial Education master’s [3]                                                              |
| College of Teacher Education          | Elementary Education; Secondary Education with English, Mathematics, or Filipino [2]; Educational Management master’s [3]                                                                                            |
| College of Maritime Education         | Marine Engineering; Marine Transportation [2]; enhanced support-level programs for deck and marine engineering [3]                                                                                                   |

The college’s undated website catalogue also lists **ABM, GAS, and Maritime senior-high strands**, plus junior high school [3].

## Choosing a program

Choose your course and specialization, then ask the college about application dates, entrance assessments, and the documents required for admission.

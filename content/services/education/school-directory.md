# Find districts and schools

Local education: open the official Siquijor school directory.

## Official directory

[Open DepEd Siquijor’s Districts and Schools page](https://www.deped-siquijor.com/schools-2/).

The directory is published by the Schools Division of Siquijor. Verify the school name and contact channel before making a trip. Enrollment deadlines, admissions requirements, and document requests should be confirmed with the school.

BetterSiquijor has not independently verified each school’s telephone number or operating hours.

# Request school records

Get a School Form 9, Good Moral Certificate, or Certificate of Enrollment from the learner’s school. Requirements and service standards below follow **DepEd’s 2026 Citizen’s Charter, 2nd Edition**. [1]

[Find your school’s location and contact details](/services/education/find-school).

## Documents to bring

- A request letter signed by the parent or guardian, stating the learner’s SF9 request: **one original and one photocopy**.
- A government-issued valid ID: **one original and one photocopy**.
- If using an authorized representative: **one original authorization letter** and **one photocopy of the requesting person’s government-issued valid ID**.

Examples of IDs listed in the charter include PhilSys ID/ePhilID, Philippine passport, driver’s license, PRC ID, UMID, voter’s ID, senior citizen ID, postal ID, PhilHealth ID, and barangay ID. [1]

## How to request

1. Submit the required documents to the school registrar or records custodian.
2. The school checks the request, verifies the record, and prepares the document for signature.
3. Wait for the school’s release notice.
4. Claim your document and sign the school’s release log.

## Fees and processing time

| Document | Fee | Charter service standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School Form 9 | None | 59 minutes |
| Good Moral Certificate or Certificate of Enrollment | None | 54 minutes |

These are the charter’s processing standards for the specified services. Ask the school about the availability of the learner’s record and collection arrangements. [1]

## Academic records for local employment

Past learners requesting certified SF9 or SF10 for local employment should bring **one original request letter** and a **government-issued valid ID, original plus one photocopy**. An authorized representative also needs an original authorization letter and a photocopy of the requesting person’s government-issued valid ID. The charter lists **no fee** and a **3-hour, 59-minute** service standard. [1]

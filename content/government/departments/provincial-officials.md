## Sources and review


The DILG snapshot supplies eight elected-member records. It does not identify their board districts or list the ex-officio board seats in this Siquijor block, so neither district assignments nor additional names are guessed here.
### Source discrepancies: two vice-mayor records

DILG’s February 2026 snapshot names **Michael Ondo Omictin** as Lazi vice mayor and **Jayson Maata Capundag** as San Juan vice mayor. DBM’s 2026 directory, printed page 233, instead lists **Rose Mae D. Dalaygon** and **Gemma Cenas**. Both readings were checked; the sources disagree. This page displays the explicitly dated DILG snapshot and does not invent a reconciliation or claim to resolve who currently holds either office.

DBM also prints Maria vice mayor as **Dondon Asok**, while the DILG snapshot gives **Meynard Real Asok**. The DILG source's full-name record is displayed above; the two source forms are retained in this note rather than silently mixed.

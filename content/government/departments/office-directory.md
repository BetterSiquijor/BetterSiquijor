## Sources and review

Governor and vice-governor addresses/emails are from the DILG February 2026 snapshot; governor-office telephone lines are from DBM 2026, printed page 191. The DILG fields `0`, `301`, and `501` are not treated as standalone telephone numbers.

Addresses and emails below come from the DILG February 2026 snapshot. The Larena mayor-office telephone comes from DBM 2026, printed page 233. These office records are separate from the provincial government.

Maria’s mayor-office source field contains **5426539**, without an area code; it is shown in the source form rather than expanded with a guessed dialing code. For the two conflicting vice-mayor names, see the discrepancy note on the [officials page](/government/departments/provincial-officials).

The House representative’s DBM source also lists **local 7362**. DBM lists **Steven J. Sumaylo** as Siquijor State College president in its 2026 directory, printed page 147. The Schools Division’s address, email, and mobile are displayed on its official division-office contact page; that page does not state a publication date.

**Source-date limitation:** the Provincial Tourism Office publishes the following office numbers, but its contact page does not give a contact-update date. They are an undated published directory snapshot retrieved on **17 November 2026**, not independently tested current emergency-response lines. Individual named staff and their mobile numbers are intentionally not mixed into this office list.

The sources above do not provide a complete appointed-department roster, current office hours, municipal transaction fees, individual case eligibility, or telephone operating status. Those fields have not been inferred or filled with dummy values. The contact record's publication date and this site's retrieval date are different things.

## Education services

- [Find a school](/services/education/find-school): search schools by name, ID, municipality, and education level.

- **Basic education enrollment:** the [local enrollment guide](/services/education/schools-division) explains the national 2025 policy, BEEF, birth-record arrangements, parent signatures, and school-to-school records transfer.
- **School documents:** the [school-records guide](/services/education/school-directory) summarises the 2026 charter’s requirements, release steps, fees, and processing standards.
- **Higher education:** the [college information page](/services/education/public-higher-education) contains Siquijor State College’s published program catalogue to help you choose a course.

## Get help with a school concern

State the school and municipality, transaction or record needed, and a safe reply channel. Ask which office handles the matter before sending confidential learner documents. This is practical preparation guidance, not an extra official documentary requirement.

# Suggest a source or correction

Help improve this independent public information portal.

## Submit a factual correction

[Open a public issue in the BetterSiquijor repository](https://github.com/BetterSiquijor/BetterSiquijor/issues/new).

Include the page URL, the statement to correct, a primary source URL, and the relevant document date or reporting period. Contributors can also propose edits through a pull request.

## Protect private information

GitHub issues are public. Do not submit identity documents, medical records, private telephone numbers, financial account details, or individual assistance applications. Contact the responsible government office directly for a personal transaction.

## Review process

A maintainer will review the cited source before changing a factual record. An issue is not an official government complaint, an emergency report, or a service application.

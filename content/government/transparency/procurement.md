## Scope and data handling

These notices belong to the **provincial government**. Municipal notices for Siquijor town, Lazi, or another LGU are excluded even where “Province of Siquijor” appears in their heading.

For reference **13258496**, the source’s delivery-period field is **0 days**. That field is preserved as printed and is not interpreted as an actual zero-day delivery commitment. Only the fields supported by the notice are included.

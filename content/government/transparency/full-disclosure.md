## How to interpret this statement

- **Receipts** report money received during the period, including non-income items such as borrowing.
- **Expenditures** are reported by service and non-operating categories; this is not a project-by-project tracker.
- **NTA** is the National Tax Allotment component, not the entire provincial budget.
- **Prior-year accounts payable** and **continuing appropriation** are shown separately before the source’s ending cash balance.
- The source’s historical region grouping is Region VII. That grouping is preserved as part of the 2024 record, not used to describe the province’s current region.
- Subtotals are copied as published. Small differences in summed components can occur because the source is rounded to 0.01 million.

The PDF notes identify DOF–BLGF and local treasurers’ submissions in the LGU Integrated Financial Tool (LIFT) as the basis for non-NTA data. NTA figures use actual LGU shares. This statement is **not an approved annual appropriation ordinance or a COA audit opinion**.

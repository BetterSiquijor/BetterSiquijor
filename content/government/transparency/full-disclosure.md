# Local financial disclosure

Official source: DILG Full Disclosure Policy Portal.

## Find the official records

[Open the DILG Full Disclosure Policy Portal](https://fdpp.dilg.gov.ph/fdpp/default). Select the province, local government, year, and document type in the source portal where available.

Financial disclosure is different from an approved local budget, an audit report, and a procurement notice. Keep the original document’s title, period, and issuing office when citing it.

## BetterSiquijor coverage

**Province-specific records have not yet been downloaded and reviewed for this portal.** No financial totals, compliance conclusions, or spending charts are published here. Failure to retrieve a document is not evidence that the LGU did not publish it.

The DILG portal can be intermittently unavailable. Try the official portal directly or request the document from the responsible office.
